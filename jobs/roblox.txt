Product Manager Intern — Roblox
San Mateo, CA

Every day, tens of millions of people come to Roblox to explore, create, and connect. Our mission is to connect a billion people with optimism and civility. We are building the future of immersive experiences together with a global community of creators.

As a Product Manager Intern, you will join a product team and help shape experiences used by millions of people around the world.

You will:
- Build product features that millions of users interact with, sharing progress and learnings with stakeholders
- Conduct user research, gather feedback, and analyze data to identify creator community needs
- Support market research and competitive analysis to inform product decisions
- Partner with senior product managers to define product strategy and roadmaps
- Write specifications, experimentation plans and go-to-market briefs
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams of engineers, designers, and data scientists

You have:
- Pursuing degree in computer science, engineering, information systems, business, mathematics or related field
- Strong analytical skills and a curiosity about user behavior
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Passionate about user experience and building products that delight users

Nice to have:
- Prior internship or project experience shipping a consumer product
- Experience with SQL or data analysis tools

Roblox does not sponsor work visas for internship positions. Candidates must be authorized to work in the United States.
