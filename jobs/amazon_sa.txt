Associate Solutions Architect Intern — Amazon (AWS)
Seattle, WA — in-person, 12-week cohort starting May 24, 2027

Do you want to help us build new things and evangelize use of AWS with customers? As an Associate Solutions Architect intern you will partner across AWS to help customers craft highly scalable, flexible and resilient cloud architectures that address customer business problems. As a trusted customer advocate, you will help organizations understand best practices around cloud-based solutions, and how to migrate existing workloads to the cloud. You will have the opportunity to help customers use AWS within their organizations ranging from new start-ups to large enterprise customers.

This position is part of an accelerated career development program for those who want to advance both their technical and business skills. The program consists of mentoring by subject matter experts, project-based learning curriculum and experiential learning assignments.

Program Overview:
The internship is a 12-week, cohort-based immersive experience that gives you a front-row seat to life as an AWS Solutions Architect. Your internship follows a weekly progression from onboarding and cloud fundamentals through hands-on customer work and a capstone project. You'll participate in customer calls, build proof-of-concept architectures, and contribute to deliverables that make a real impact.

Location Details: This program is offered only in Seattle, WA.
Start Date Details: Start dates are tentatively set to occur May 24th, 2027.

Key job responsibilities:
- Create solutions that address customer business problems and accelerate the adoption of AWS services.
- Interface with customer stakeholders, including developers and team leads.
- Drive effective feedback gathering from customers, distill and translate feedback into clear business and technical requirements for product and engineering teams.

Basic Qualifications:
- Work 40 hours/week minimum and commit to a 12 week internship maximum.
- Experience with one of the following programming languages: Python, Ruby, Node.js, C#, or C++.
- Experience with AI/ML technologies.
- Experience with one or more of the following domains: analytics, security, storage, DevOps, application development, or machine learning.
- Experience in written and verbal communication skills to communicate with technical and non-technical audiences, including senior leadership.
- Currently enrolled in a bachelors or greater degree program in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or related technical fields with a graduation date between December 2027 and September 2028.
- Are 18 years of age or older.

Preferred Qualifications:
- AWS or other cloud certification.
- Experience applying established frameworks and design patterns to deliver working software (can include coursework, personal projects, hackathons, or previous internships).
