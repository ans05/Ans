Solutions Architect Intern — AGS Tech U (AWSI), 2027 — Amazon
Seattle, WA — in-person

Amazon Global Sales (AGS) Tech U is a 12-week, cohort-based internship starting May 24, 2027 in Seattle, designed to build the next generation of Solutions Architects. Interns rotate through hands-on technical training and shadow customer-facing engagements across one or more technical domains.

What you'll do:
- Learn to design and present technical solutions across domains such as analytics, security, storage, DevOps, application development, or machine learning.
- Work with AI/ML technologies as part of customer-facing technical scenarios.
- Communicate technical recommendations to technical and non-technical audiences, including senior leadership.
- Participate in a structured, cohort-based technical curriculum alongside other interns.

Basic Qualifications:
- Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or greater degree program, with an expected graduation date between December 2027 and September 2028.
- Programming experience in Python, Ruby, Node.js, C#, or C++.
- Experience with AI/ML technologies.
- Experience in one or more of: analytics, security, storage, DevOps, application development, or machine learning.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to communicate with senior leadership.
- Must be 18 years of age or older.
- Able to work in person in Seattle, WA for the full 12-week cohort starting May 24, 2027.

Amazon is an equal opportunity employer and does not discriminate on the basis of protected veteran status, disability, or other legally protected status.
