Product Analytics Intern — Notion
San Francisco, CA

The Product Analytics team helps Notion's product teams make better decisions with data. You'll partner with product managers and engineers to define metrics, build dashboards, and analyze experiments.

What you'll do:
- Write SQL to build and maintain dashboards tracking product KPIs such as activation, retention, and conversion
- Analyze A/B tests and report results and recommendations to product managers
- Define success metrics for new features with product and design partners
- Investigate funnel drop-offs and user behavior patterns to identify opportunities

What we're looking for:
- Strong SQL skills and experience with Python or R for analysis
- Experience with experimentation or A/B testing
- Familiarity with a BI tool such as Looker, Tableau, or Mode
- Clear written communication; able to explain analyses to non-technical partners

Nice to have:
- Experience with product analytics tools (Amplitude, Mixpanel)
