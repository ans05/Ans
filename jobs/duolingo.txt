You have...
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's degree in a technical or related field (e.g., Computer Science, HCI, Data Science, Business) with an expected graduation date of Fall 2027 or Spring/Summer 2028
- Demonstrated ability to be inventive and innovative
- An instinct for creating simple and intuitive user experiences
- Ability to utilize user insights, data, and statistical analyses to inform decisions
- Clear communication skills and the ability to explain complex concepts simply
- Shown ability to learn fast
- Demonstrated leadership skills
- A desire to make the world a better place through technology and education

Exceptional candidates will have...
- A GPA of 3.5+
- A Duolingo streak
